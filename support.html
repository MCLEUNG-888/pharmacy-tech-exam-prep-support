Support

Help with the app, content, and purchases.

Email MCDEVTEAM1@OUTLOOK.COM for help with Pharmacy Tech Exam Prep. Tell us what happened so we can investigate.

What to include

  • Your device model, iOS version, and app version.
  • Whether the app is set to English or Spanish.
  • The exact steps to reproduce the issue and what you expected.
  • Screenshots if helpful.
  • The question ID if you are reporting a content issue.

Protect private information

Do not send passwords, payment-card numbers, pharmacy patient data, protected health information (PHI), or prescription or customer confidential information.

Try these steps

  1. Close and reopen the app.
  2. Install the latest available app update and iOS update.
  3. Restart your device and check available storage.
  4. If a subscription purchase is missing, open the app’s Premium screen and select Restore Purchases. Use the Apple account associated with the purchase. This restores an eligible purchase, not local study progress.

Subscription billing

Apple handles App Store billing. You can manage or cancel an Apple subscription in your device’s subscription settings. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. If Restore Purchases does not resolve access, email us with the app version and what the app displays; do not send a full receipt or payment-card details.

Content or accessibility feedback

Please include the question ID for a content report, or describe the screen and assistive technology involved for an accessibility report. We cannot provide individual exam eligibility or certification decisions; verify official requirements directly with PTCB.
