Privacy Policy

How Pharmacy Tech Exam Prep handles information.

This policy describes the current iOS app and this support website. The app is an independent PTCE study tool. For questions, email MCDEVTEAM1@OUTLOOK.COM.

Information in the app

The current app saves your English or Spanish setting and an active practice session on your device. A session may include selected questions, answers, and session progress. The app does not provide an account or developer cloud sync for this study data. The current app does not send this study data to a developer-operated server.

Accounts, analytics, ads, and tracking

The current app does not require an account and does not include developer-operated analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking in its reviewed implementation. It does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission. We will update this policy if those practices change.

App Store purchases

The app includes optional subscription purchase and Restore Purchases controls. Availability, price, billing, and transaction processing are handled through Apple’s App Store and your Apple account, subject to Apple’s terms and privacy practices. We do not receive your full payment-card number through the app. The app receives purchase status information needed to show the purchase or restoration result; the reviewed PTCE implementation keeps its active subscription status in memory during the app session. Removing local study data does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Support messages

If you email support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. We use it to respond to your request and handle related follow-up. Email is handled by the mail providers involved; please avoid sending sensitive pharmacy or payment information. You can ask us to delete your support correspondence at MCDEVTEAM1@OUTLOOK.COM. We may need to retain information where required by law or to resolve an ongoing request.

This website

These static pages have no contact form, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or cookies set by site code. GitHub Pages, your browser, and your network provider may process technical request data under their own practices when you visit the site.

Your choices

You can change the language setting in the app. See Data & deletion for local data removal and purchase restoration guidance. Contact us with a privacy question or deletion request at MCDEVTEAM1@OUTLOOK.COM.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The date below shows the latest site revision.
