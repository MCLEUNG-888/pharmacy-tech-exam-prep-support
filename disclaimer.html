Independent App & Exam Disclaimer

Understand what this app is and what it cannot promise.

Independent educational tool

Pharmacy Tech Exam Prep is an independent educational study tool. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, endorsed by, or officially published by the Pharmacy Technician Certification Board (PTCB).

Practice content

Practice questions are independently authored. They are not official PTCE exam questions, and the app does not reproduce or claim access to live exam questions.

Outcomes and official requirements

Using this app does not guarantee passing the PTCE, certification, employment, licensure, or eligibility. Exam policies, eligibility, exam structure, and certification requirements can change. Verify them directly with PTCB before making decisions about an application or exam appointment.

Questions

For app content issues, contact MCDEVTEAM1@OUTLOOK.COM and include the question ID. We cannot speak for PTCB or resolve official exam decisions.
