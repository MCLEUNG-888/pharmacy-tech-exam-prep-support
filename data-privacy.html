Data & Privacy / Deletion

Control the study information stored on your device.

The reviewed iOS app stores the English or Spanish setting and the active practice session on your device using local app preferences. The app does not offer developer cloud sync or an account for study progress.

What can be removed in the app

Starting a new practice session with a different domain replaces the unfinished session after you confirm in the app. The current app has no separate user-facing “delete all study data” control. The app’s internal session store can clear a session, but that control is not exposed as a general reset action.

Delete local app data

To remove the app and its on-device app data, use iOS Settings → General → iPhone Storage → Pharmacy Tech Exam Prep → Delete App, or remove the app from the Home Screen and choose Delete App. “Offload App” can retain app data, so use Delete App if your goal is to remove local app data. A device backup may have a separate copy governed by your Apple backup settings.

Reinstalling and purchases

There is no developer cloud restore for study progress. Do not rely on a reinstall or a new device to recover a local practice session. App Store subscriptions are separate from local study data. The app includes Restore Purchases in its Premium screen for eligible purchases associated with your Apple account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription; manage it through Apple.

Support correspondence

To request deletion of emails you sent to support, write to MCDEVTEAM1@OUTLOOK.COM from the same email address and identify the messages you want removed. We may need to retain information required by law or to resolve an ongoing request. Do not send patient information, PHI, card numbers, or other confidential pharmacy information.
